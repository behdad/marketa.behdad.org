# The Loft Game Manual

Loft Day is a point-and-click visit to ten connected rooms. Its story unfolds in three parts, with
games, music, messages, apps, guests, and smaller surprises along the way.

No adventure-game experience is required. Read the caption below the scene and try whatever looks
useful.

## Start here

1. Select **CLICK ME**. On a narrow phone, landscape orientation works best.
2. Use the opening coach’s visible ×, `Enter`, Escape, or Backspace twice while it points out the
   top navigation, then the bottom caption. Other scene clicks stay paused until those two steps
   are acknowledged.
3. Follow the caption below the scene. Press `Enter` whenever you want the next guided action.

## How the day unfolds

### Morning routine

Follow the caption through the five main rooms. Each unfinished room remembers its next step:

- **Kitchen:** make an espresso.
- **Garden:** care for a plant, add music, and set the light.
- **Cuddly-puddly:** follow Octi's lead.
- **Office:** use both computers, then settle the room for the evening.
- **Balcony:** finish the clue trail and open the rest of the loft.

Once the Balcony is complete, the lower floor and the room map are yours to explore.

### Party

Return to the Garden and start the party. From then on, one selection in **The Loft** room map can
unlock and open any room you have not visited. See all ten rooms; the captions and room map keep
count so you do not need to remember them yourself.

A coach points to the Garden wall switch when it is time to wind the party down. You may use it or
keep dancing while you explore. After all ten rooms have been seen, a four-message exchange leads
to **Let’s go!**; selecting it ends any active party and takes you to the Entrance. If you miss the
messages, selecting the Entrance road does the same wind-down. Starting the party at least once is
part of the story—room shortcuts alone do not unlock the trip.

### Road Trip

The first drive begins with a short car coach. Take a street lap if you like, then use the
**Road Trip** control to choose Calgary, Banff, or Abraham Lake. The final route includes an
optional Camping turnoff. Build a fire, make stew, stargaze, and put the fire out when prompted to
reach the `~ fin ~` scene.

## Moving between rooms

Each main room has a room directly below it:

| Main floor | Lower floor |
| --- | --- |
| Kitchen | Bathroom |
| Garden | Dungeon |
| Cuddly-puddly | Cinema |
| Office | Bedroom |
| Balcony | Entrance |

- Select the side arrows or room dots to travel on the main floor.
- Press `←` or `→` to move one room at a time.
- Press `1`–`5` to jump to Kitchen through Balcony upstairs.
- Press `6`–`0` to jump to Bathroom through Entrance downstairs.
- Press `↓` twice to deliberately unlock the lower floor; after discovery, press it once to enter
  the paired lower room. Press `↑` to return.
- While downstairs, `←` and `→` move along the lower floor.
- `Tab` or the window-grid button opens **The Loft** room map. Arrow keys select an already
  discovered room and `Enter` opens it. On a locked room, press `Enter` twice quickly to unlock it.
  Discovered rooms are sharp; the others are blurred; double-click or double-tap one to unlock it
  deliberately. Once the party has begun, one click, tap, or `Enter` unlocks and opens any
  remaining locked card. Its first card is Kitchen / Bar.
  During a Road Trip or at Camping, the Entrance card mirrors that live scene. Opening the map
  during a Road Trip leaves the drive paused when the map closes.
- The window-grid button remains available beside the room dots. The floor button stays visible
  but disabled until a downstairs route has been discovered; double-click or double-tap it to
  unlock that route deliberately.
- Escape, Backspace, ×, or the top-left arrow dismisses the current layer. From an idle lower room,
  use `↑` or the Up button to return upstairs.

Each main room also has an in-scene route downstairs; finding one unlocks lower-floor navigation.

## Objects and shortcuts

A click or tap is the main discovery gesture. Some objects can also be dragged; a short tap still
uses their ordinary action. Right-click opens local actions. On touch, press and hold without
moving.

Press `?` outside a typing field for the complete shortcut card. The essentials are:

- `←` / `→`: adjacent room
- `↓` / `↑`: downstairs or upstairs
- `Tab`: open or close **The Loft** room map
- `1`–`5`: upstairs room
- `6`–`0`: downstairs room
- `Enter`: next guided action; after the morning routine, the room's main activity (in Garden /
  Party, toggle the party)
- `C`: phone
- `/`: Messages
- `M`: Office monitor
- `L`: Office laptop
- `D`: day or night
- `Space`: current game action or media play/pause
- `F`: browser fullscreen
- Escape / Backspace: dismiss or go back
- `R`: Restart, with confirmation

Typing fields, open devices, and active games keep the keys they need. If the morning routine is
stuck, press `Enter`. As an explicit skip, double-click or double-tap a locked destination dot, or
press its room number.

## Things to explore

The five main rooms feature **Flair Catch**, **Hack-Man**, **Octi's Escape**, **Alien Resources**,
and **Block Party**. Their opening screen or caption explains the controls.

The lower rooms offer:

- **Bathroom:** fill the tub to start the 20-second Bubble-gun round. Click bubbles for one point,
  or drag the stool until its guide locks onto a bubble and release to fire for two. The round's ×
  or Escape/Backspace leaves the Bathroom open.
- **Dungeon:** select the play wall to start or resume Prince of Persia. Escape leaves the
  presentation without discarding the run.
- **Cinema:** the projector wakes on your first visit; choose a film and use its playback controls.
  Bare Enter or Space wakes it later, then starts the selected film or pauses/resumes the playing one. Escape,
  Backspace, or the screen × stops a film and returns to the chooser; press Escape or Backspace
  again to turn off the projector.
- **Bedroom:** select a stained-glass pane to start tic-tac-toe. After a result, clear the board and
  select again for a new game; Escape or Backspace clears the current board without leaving.
- **Entrance:** select the road to open the Porsche dashboard and driving game. During the Garden
  party, select the shaking second window from the left to return directly to the party.

The phone and Office monitor contain more games and tools, including Classics, Nibbles, Pac-Man,
shooters, Code, Python, Linux, music, video, camera, calendar, and drawing apps. Smaller surprises
are intentionally left uncatalogued.

## Phone, Messages, and computers

Open the phone with `C`, `/` for Messages, or its in-scene control. The first ordinary opening has
a small math lock; three failed attempts unlock it. Home, Back, and Recents follow familiar phone
behavior.

On phones and tablets, opening or restoring an app leaves text fields unfocused so the software
keyboard does not cover the app. Tap the specific search, message, notes, browser, code, chat, or
console field when you want to type. Desktop app openings retain their ready-to-type behavior.

Opening a message does not automatically run its activity. Select the activity arrow when ready.
An activity can expire when its moment passes, but its message stays until you read it.

Party coaches identify the Garden wall switch and, if needed, the room map. Their × dismisses the
lesson without ending the party, and room navigation remains available. New notifications and calls
wait until a coach is out of the way. Lower-room introductions report how many rooms remain. After
all ten have been visited, Behdad and Markéta’s Road Trip exchange arrives in order; only
**Let’s go!** has an activity arrow. Camping's `~ fin ~` is the story's RSVP coda.

Right-click or long-press a message or app icon for local actions. **Kill app** resets only that app.

The Office laptop handles calls. The larger monitor contains desktop apps and games. Select the
monitor to zoom, then select its screen. Open Search with an empty query for the app directory;
type to filter, use the arrows to choose, and press Enter. Closing an app preserves supported state,
while **Kill app** starts it fresh.

The monitor's far-right taskbar control expands it inside the browser. This is separate from browser
fullscreen.

## Driving and Road Trip

Select the Entrance road to open the dashboard. The caption below the scene reports the current
driving state; the visible dashboard controls and shortcuts operate the car. A first-drive coach
walks through ignition, steering, gear, desktop cruise control, and pedals; `?` parks the car and
restarts that lesson.

On desktop:

- press `Enter` while the engine is off, or select the ignition key;
- select **AUTO** or **MANUAL**, press `A` / `M`, or use `Ctrl`+`←` / `Ctrl`+`→`;
- steer with `←` / `→`; holding or repeated taps steer more strongly;
- accelerate with `↑` and brake with `↓`;
- tap `Ctrl` to hold or reset cruising speed; brake to cancel;
- in AUTO, use `Shift`+`↓` toward D and `Shift`+`↑` toward P;
- in MANUAL, use `Shift`+`↑` / `Shift`+`↓` to change gear.

On touch, start the engine to reveal blue steering and pink accelerator/brake sliders, or use the
physical wheel, pedals, mode buttons, and shifter. The pink slider's middle holds speed; its outer
zones accelerate or brake, and lifting your finger holds the release speed as cruise. AUTO offers
P/R/N/D; in MANUAL, long-press the shifter to choose an exact gear.

The roof, door, windows, compartments, and lights are separate controls. The odometer records real
distance across street and highway driving and survives engine stops and Continue.

Starting the party and seeing all ten rooms qualifies **Road Trip**. The exchange’s **Let’s go!**
ends any active party before opening Entrance. If the exchange is ignored, selecting the road or
pressing `Enter` there performs the same wind-down before opening the dashboard. Complete or dismiss
the first-drive coach, then accept the invitation and choose Calgary, Banff, or Abraham Lake. Street
laps are optional free driving; each new route starts stopped on the shoulder without changing the
selected transmission setting.
Closing the dashboard, Entrance, or page does not consume the unlock.

Road Trip uses the same steering, transmission, accelerator, and brake:

- Calgary is a straight divided highway with a 110 km/h limit.
- The Banff leg narrows, curves through the mountains, and has a 90 km/h limit.
- The Abraham Lake road has one lane each way, wildlife, lake views, and a 100 km/h limit. Its
  Camping turnoff is optional: move onto the right shoulder when the sign and side road arrive.

Launching a route temporarily suspends a running upstairs party, including its music and autonomous
guest moments. Parking or exiting the route restores it; merely opening the dashboard does not
suspend it.

AUTO provides gentle curve assistance; MANUAL leaves curve steering to the player. Natural traffic
paces itself to the posted road; faster drivers pass when clear and queue when they cannot. Shoulders,
gravel, weather, wildlife, and police affect the trip. Leaving or unfocusing an active highway pauses
it. Select **Road Trip** → **Continue** to restore the run, **New** to replace it after choosing a
segment, or **Camping** to revisit the campsite once reached. Continue reopens paused; Play or fresh
steering or pedal input resumes it.

Police tolerate 20 km/h above the posted limit, and an oncoming car may flash before a speed trap.
If police pursue, stop safely. The driver panel tracks **Demerits** and a simplified **BAC**;
demerits expire after three real minutes and one drink-equivalent clears per real minute.

Distance, clean passes, avoided wildlife, and pickups earn points. Clean actions build a combo up
to `3×`; collisions reset it and can damage the windshield, stall the engine, or knock the
transmission into neutral.

Taking the Camping turnoff slows the car automatically. If you stay on the through lane, the exit
returns later. At camp, select the empty pit and add tinder, kindling, and either log arrangement. A
pinecone dropped from the trees can replace the twigs; missing fuel burns out.

The fire stays lit through dinner and stargazing and can be put out only when the sleep suggestion
asks for it. Continue retains a completed fire. Escape, Backspace, or the top-right × returns to the
Entrance dashboard; `Enter` stays in camp and performs one next step—build and light the fire, start
and cook a valid stew, serve it, then finish stargazing. Key repeat and quick double presses are
ignored.

The lit fire reveals a food crate. Build a stew from one protein, one base, and the pantry items,
then cook it in the camp pot. The lid lets you peek but slows cooking. After serving, select the sky
to trace Cassiopeia, Ursa Major, and Ursa Minor by selecting or dragging through each glowing star
in order; `Enter` completes the trace directly. The solved stars remain draggable beneath the
northern lights.

After the closing campfire exchange, click anywhere or press `Enter`, then put out the fire when the
caption asks. The campers retire, the bears handle the leftovers, and the view rises into the finale.
Its caption includes the time spent in loft and invites you to share it in the RSVP. Leaving pauses
this curtain call; returning continues it. A later Camping visit begins again at an empty firepit.

## Help, saves, and browser notes

The caption guides the morning routine, then becomes room or activity feedback. One-time coaches
point out a few easy-to-miss controls elsewhere in the game.

The game begins checkpointing after the Kitchen is solved or deliberately left for another room.
On a later visit, **Continue** restores the saved room and floor, progression, durable arrangements,
Album and Messages data. Running games, calls, cameras, and media return stopped. The finale clock
counts only while the started game is visible, focused, and not on a paused Road Trip; Continue
resumes that total. Rooms you had not reached remain locked. **Start over** clears the checkpoint and its clock; Restart
inside the game asks for confirmation.

**Trailer** is a separate one-minute preview. It does not consume a save and offers **Take over** to
start the morning routine.

- English and Czech are available, and the choice persists when storage is allowed.
- Reduced-motion preferences simplify animation and disable Flair Catch.
- Camera, torch, sharing, fullscreen, installation, and some computer toys depend on browser support
  and permission; denying them does not block the story.
- Desktop Chrome has the broadest feature coverage, but other modern browsers remain playable.
- If audio is silent, interact with the page once so the browser can enable sound.

The simplest path: finish the morning routine, start the party, visit all ten rooms, then hit the road.
